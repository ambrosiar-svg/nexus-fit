How it works

  1. Pick VIP or Preferred on this page.
  2. Create your member login (that's your book of what's left). Card billing is Stripe.
  3. The shop records each visit. You see remaining services. You cannot add or remove a service yourself.

Monthly plans renew month-to-month; cancel effective at the end of the current billing period. Annual plans renew unless you send written non-renewal at least 30 days before the anniversary. 30-day cancel: keep completed work at member pricing; unused services end.
